Ordering

How to order

Export from our Hwaseong office. Quotations are issued per order and confirmed by email.

  1. Select products

    Choose products and check the quantity against the MOQ shown for each item.

  2. Request a quotation

    Send your list to Sathish by phone or message. We reply with pricing and lead time.

  3. Confirm and pay deposit

    The order is confirmed on deposit. Packaging and private label options are agreed in writing.

  4. Production and shipping

    Manufactured in Korea, packed for export and shipped FOB with full documentation.
