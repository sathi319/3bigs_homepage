Leadership Team

Scientists who lead the company

KM
Kim Kyung-minGeneral Manager

Kwangmin Kim is a specialist in personalized medicine, bioinformatics, and microbiome research with over eight years of experience transforming complex biological data into actionable healthcare insights. He holds a Master’s degree in Genetics and Biomedical Informatics from Pusan National University School of Medicine and joined 3BIGS as a core member in 2018.

Throughout his career, he has contributed to numerous scientific publications and collaborative research projects with leading institutions, including Seoul National University School of Dentistry, Yonsei University Health System, and LG Household & Health Care. His expertise spans microbiome data analysis, biomedical informatics, and large-scale biological data interpretation.

More recently, Kwangmin has expanded his work from research and analysis into microbiome database and platform development. By integrating microbiome, clinical, lifestyle, and health-related data, he is helping build advanced data-driven and AI-powered solutions designed to accelerate personalized healthcare and precision medicine.

NS
Natarajan Sathishkumar, Ph.DCTO & R&BD Director

Dr.N.Sathishkumar is a scientific and technology leader specializing in translating advanced bioinformatics, multi-omics, and microbiome research into scalable products, platforms, partnerships, and commercial solutions.

Currently serving as CTO and Director of R&BD, he leads the development of direct-to-consumer (DTC) microbiome-based products and services across NGS applications. His work spans bioinformatics pipeline development, advanced downstream analytics, benchmarking and reference databases, web-based platforms, automated reporting, and end-to-end sample-to-insight workflows, alongside scientific strategy, cross-functional R&D, international partnerships, and business development.

He holds a Ph.D. in Biotechnology from Kyung Hee University, South Korea, with postdoctoral research experience at Sunchon National University. With 14+ years of experience, more than 60 peer-reviewed scientific publications, patents, and recognition among the Top 5% of Scientists Worldwide in the 2025 SciRank Global ranking, his work bridges scientific research, technology development, and real-world biotechnology innovation.

How to Use

A simple daily ritual

1 · Mask pack — 2–3 times a week
Cleanse, then apply the Peptide Repair Face Mask to fit the face. Leave for 10–20 minutes, remove, and gently pat the remaining essence into the skin.
2 · Serum — morning and evening
After toning, apply 2–3 drops of Derma Silk Serum to the face and press gently until absorbed. Use before cream.
3 · Cream — morning and evening
Warm a small amount of Derma Silk Cream between the fingers and smooth over the face and neck as the final step of your routine.
Recommendations for sensitive skin
Patch-test on the inner forearm before first use. If irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult a dermatologist. Store away from direct sunlight.

What it is

Neurofeedback, made personal

Neurofeedback is a form of brain training in which real-time information about your own brainwave activity is fed back to you. Over repeated sessions, the brain learns to recognise and reshape its own patterns.

NeuroHarmony is a portable, dry-electrode headband developed in Korea and cleared as a two-channel EEG device. 3BIGS delivers the programme — measurement, structured training and progress reporting — alongside its bio-data analysis services.

EEG SIGNALBRAIN WELLNESSPersonalized Report
How it works

Four steps to a quieter mind

Measure

The headband records brainwave signals from dry gold-plated electrodes — no gel, no paste, no clinic visit.

Analyse

Signals are decomposed into delta, theta, alpha, beta and gamma bands and compared with reference norms.

Train

Guided neurofeedback sessions reward the brain states you want more of — calm, focus or relaxation.

Track

Session-by-session reports show change over time, so progress is measurable rather than anecdotal.

Brainwave Bands

What we measure

DeltaDeep, dreamless sleep and restorative rest.
ThetaDrowsiness, deep relaxation and creative reverie.
AlphaCalm, wakeful relaxation — a key indicator in cognitive-ageing research.
BetaActive thinking, alertness and focused attention.
GammaHigh-level information processing and binding of sensory input.
Applications

Where NeuroHarmony is used

Focus & attention

Training sessions that support sustained attention and self-regulation, used in educational and clinical settings.

Stress & relaxation

Alpha-oriented training to support calm under pressure and faster recovery from stress.

Healthy cognitive ageing

Programmes for older adults targeting cognitive function and sensory processing — supported by peer-reviewed research.

Sleep quality

Relaxation protocols and evening routines designed around slow-wave and alpha activity.

Clinics & wellness centres

A compact device that brings measured brain training into a practice without imaging-room infrastructure.

Research

Quantitative EEG endpoints for studies in cognition, ageing and speech perception, with data-analysis support from 3BIGS.
